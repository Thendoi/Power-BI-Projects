# Power-BI-Projects

Power BI Insurance Analytics Dashboard
Project Title : Insurance Claims & Policy Insights Dashboard
Tool: Microsoft Power BI

Project Title / Headline .📊 Prism Insurance Analytics Dashboard A complete, interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze insurance policies, claims, customer demographics, premium distribution, and operational performance. This visualization helps stakeholders track business KPIs, monitor claim trends, and make data-driven decisions.

Short Description / Purpose The Prism Insurance Analytics Dashboard provides a unified view of critical insurance metrics across multiple categories including claim amounts, policy types, premium distribution, customer details, and claim statuses. This dashboard supports business analysts, insurance managers, risk officers, and decision-makers to quickly understand business performance, identify trends, and evaluate operational efficiency.

Tech Stack This dashboard was developed using the following tools & technologies: .📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization and reporting tool .📂 Power Query – Data transformation, cleaning & preprocessing .🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Measures, KPIs, calculated fields .🧱 Data Modeling – Relationships between claims, customers, and policy tables .📁 File Formats – .pbix (dashboard), .png (report preview)

Data Source Source: Sample Insurance Dataset (policies, claims, customers, premiums) The dataset includes information such as: . Policy details (type, status, coverage, premium) . Claim data (claim amount, claim status, claim number) . Customer demographics (age group, gender, ID) . Insurance product categories (Life, Auto, Health, Travel, etc.) The data is structured across multiple tables supporting cross-filtering and multi-dimensional analysis.

Features / Highlights 🧩 Business Problem Insurance companies often struggle with: . Understanding claim trends . Identifying high-risk policy types . Monitoring premium performance . Analyzing customer segments . Tracking rejected vs settled claims Without a dashboard, answering questions like: . Which policies generate the highest premiums? . Which age groups submit the highest claims? . What percentage of claims are rejected or settled? becomes time-consuming and error-prone.

🎯 Goal of the Dashboard This dashboard aims to: . Provide a consolidated view of insurance operations . Help identify trends in claims and premiums . Support strategic planning using customer and policy insights . Offer dynamic filtering for claim number, customer ID, and policy details . Improve decision-making for business users

🔎 Walkthrough of Key Visuals

Premium Amount by Policy Type (Bar Chart)
Displays total premium collected across Auto, Health, Life, and Travel insurance categories.

Coverage Amount Overview (Area Chart)
Shows overall coverage amounts across major policy categories.

Claim Amount by Age Group (Column Chart)
Identifies which age segments (Young Adults, Adults, Elders) generate higher claim payouts.

Customer Gender Distribution (Donut Chart)
Visual illustration of male vs female policyholders.

Policy Status — Active vs Inactive (Donut Chart)
Shows operational status of policies across the company.

Claims by Status (Clustered Column Chart)
Compares settled, rejected, and pending claims to reveal claim performance.

Claim Values by Policy Type (Table)
Interactive table showing pending and rejected claim amounts across policy types.

Slicers for Policy/Customer/Claim Users can filter dashboard by: . Policy Number . Customer ID . Claim Number This enables granular, customer-level or claim-level analysis.
📈 Business Impact & Insights Key Use Cases: . Operational Monitoring: Track insurance claim trends and bottlenecks

. Risk Assessment: Identify high-risk age groups and policy types

. Revenue Tracking: Understand which policies generate more premiums

. Customer Segmentation: Analyze gender and age distribution for marketing

. Process Improvement: Compare settled vs rejected claim ratios

Insights Uncovered:

. Majority of claims come from adults in mid-age groups

. Certain policy types show higher rejection or pending claims

. Gender distribution provides actionable demographic insights

. Some policies generate high premiums but low claim occurrences

. Coverage and premium trends differ widely by policy category
